# CareRisk 48H — Evidence & Abstention Explorer

This public Space is a synthetic-only evidence and abstention explorer. It demonstrates gate states, missingness, and permitted research output using built-in scenarios. It does not accept, store, or process user-provided patient data.

## Evidence

The evidence shown here is a research demonstration, not clinical validation or a care recommendation. The Space is not a regulated product and must not be used to guide a person's care or treatment.

## Safety boundary

- No patient data entry or upload.
- No live predictions, risk classes, case recommendations, threshold-based case decisions, triage, resource allocation, diagnosis, or treatment.
- Synthetic gate-state scenarios are the only supported inputs.
- Apache-2.0 covers the application code. PhysioNet data is absent from this Space and remains under its source license; no PhysioNet data is bundled here.
